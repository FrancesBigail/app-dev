## Favorite series: BBC Sherlock Holmes
**Small information about my favorite series:**
**BBC Sherlock** is a British television series and a contemporary version of the original Sir Arthur Conan Doyle's Sherlock Holmes [detective stories](https://bakerstreet.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Sherlock_Holmes_Short_Stories_and_Novels). It started on 2010 before ending on 2017 with 4 series containing 3 90-minute episodes each. This was a very popular show back then alongside other two big British shows: Supernatural and Dr. Who, which at the time were the big three British shows that fans had loved and was popular on the website, Tumblr. Sherlock Holmes _(played by Benedict Cumberbatch)_, the world's only "consulting detective", and John Watson _(played by Martin Freeman)_, a former Army doctor and Sherlock's best friend and assistant, work together to solve and uncover the many mysteries that surround London, Britain.
